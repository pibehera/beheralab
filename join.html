Join Us

We welcome students and researchers interested in atomic-scale materials and future electronic technologies.

Apply

Postdoctoral researchers

We do not currently have funded postdoctoral positions available. Candidates pursuing external fellowships or other independent funding are welcome to email with a CV and brief description of their research interests.

Current or admitted UCLA graduate students

Students interested in joining the group are encouraged to email with a CV and a short description of their research interests.

Undergraduate students

UCLA undergraduates interested in research opportunities are welcome to email with a CV and transcript.

UCLA also offers several programs supporting undergraduate research through the Undergraduate Research Portal and the UCLA Samueli Undergraduate Research Program.

Prospective Ph.D. students

Please apply directly to the UCLA Materials Science and Engineering graduate program. Admission decisions are made through the department’s graduate admissions process. Due to the volume of inquiries, individual emails regarding admission status or application review may not receive a response.
