Functional Materials for Energy-Efficient Electronics

We translate functional materials into new electronic capabilities by coupling ferroelectric order with charge, spin, and heat transport. These interactions can enable new approaches to energy-efficient logic and memory, multifunctional electronics, and integration with thermal, magnetic, superconducting, and quantum technologies.
