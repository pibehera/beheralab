What we explore

Research

We develop atomically precise materials and interfaces, then study how their structure and properties can enable new electronic technologies.

01

Atomic-Scale Synthesis

Atomic layer deposition and thin-film synthesis provide precise control of electronic materials, heterostructures, and the interfaces between them.

02

Ferroelectrics & Dielectrics

We investigate ultrathin ferroelectrics, negative permittivity, and advanced gate dielectrics for energy-efficient electronic systems.

03

Emerging Electronic Materials & Interconnects

We explore alternative conductors, unconventional electronic materials, and conformal synthesis strategies for increasingly scaled structures.

04

Quantum & Cryogenic Electronics

We engineer dielectric and electronic materials for operation at cryogenic temperatures and in quantum and low-temperature systems.
